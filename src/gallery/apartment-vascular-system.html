{{title}}

The series traces the interior life of domestic space. In each work, red vascular forms move through rooms — connecting plants, furniture, and architectural remnants as though the apartment itself has a circulatory system.

Objects here are not passive: they hold time, carry memory of lives that preceded the current inhabitant, and remain linked to a world before the self. The series proposes the home as a living body, and dwelling as a form of mutual absorption.
